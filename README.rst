`EXPERIMENTAL DATA`_ 
------

New experimental measurements of the H\ :sub:`2` CIA fundamental band in the [3600, 5500] cm\ :sup:`-1` spectral range, using both a pure H\ :sub:`2` gas and a H\ :sub:`2`-He mixture, have been published in [Vitali2025]_. The H\ :sub:`2`-H\ :sub:`2` and H\ :sub:`2`-He binary absorption coefficients have been recorded for temperatures going from 120 to 504 K, at a resolution of 1 cm\ :sup:`-1`. The data can be downloaded in tabular form from Zenodo platform at this link `<https://zenodo.org/records/13142015>` and have been added in the HITRAN database as mentioned in the last HITRAN CIA update paper (see [Terragni2025]_)
